# MiG-29 9.13 flight model

Point-mass card for the single-seat MiG-29, изделие **9.13** (Fulcrum-C). Geometry, including the shared planform, is [fighters/mig-29-9-13.md](../fighters/mig-29-9-13.md). This file does not copy Su-27 thrust, mass, or Mach. It does not invent Cd0 or CLmax.

Overall length **17.32 m** with the pitot, span **11.36 m**, and leading-edge sweep **42°** are the shared basic-MiG-29 size in that geometry note. They are not a 9.13-only outline. The 9.13 change used here is internal fuel, not a new length. The dorsal spine is the bay that was drawn for an overlay tank and then used for the Gardeniya jammer. Fuselage tank No. 1 was only rearranged, **+240 л**. That 240 L is not a length and it is not a second tank stacked on top of the 4540 L total.

Conflicting prints are separate rows. They are not averaged. A 9.12, version B, or MiG-29SE mass is not used as a 9.13 mass. Where that is the only table a page prints, the variant is in the row.

## Status

**BROCHURE_ONLY.**

Opened pages give weights, litres, static thrust, brochure speed, ceiling, climb, and one G figure. They do not give a drag polar, Cd0, CLmax, a thrust-lapse table, or an inertia tensor. One booklet copies an operational-limitations list, but the angle-of-attack item is damaged and does not name 9.13. That is not a partial polar.

## Variant

Model the series 9.13: one seat, production from 1986, RD-33 as printed below, spine and the +240 L tank rearrangement. Do not substitute these airframes or ratings:

- **9.12.** Booklet column empty mass **10 900 kg**, maximum takeoff **18 100 kg**, internal fuel in the prose **4300 л** (the figure before the 9-13 parenthetical). Not the 9.13 rows.
- **MiG-29 version B and MiG-29SE.** RAC’s page. Version B standard takeoff **14 900 kg**, maximum **18 000 kg**. SE standard **15 300 kg**, maximum **20 000 kg**, ceiling **17 750 m**. The page never prints 9.13. SE’s “increased internal fuel” is not a 9.13 fuel mass.
- **9.13S / MiG-29S.** Same booklet, third column: normal takeoff **15 600 kg**, maximum **19 700 kg**, warload **4000 kg**. The SOS sentence’s **28°** is named only for MiG-29S, MiG-29SE, and MiG-29SD.
- **RD-33K and RD-33MK.** Emergency or uprated thrusts (**8700 кгс**, **9000 кгс**) belong to those engines. Aircraft No. 1616 was later fitted with an RD-33K for ground runs. That is not the series 9.13 rating.

The 9.13 aircraft article prints **РД-33** with no series dash. Klimov’s series 3 is an increased-life engine on the same thrust table, not a new rating. RAC’s **RD-33 ser.2 (3)** is the version B / UB / SE cell, not a statement that a 1986 9.13 left the factory on series 3. No opened page assigns a dash to the baseline 9.13.

## Point-mass card

Wing loadings and thrust-to-weight ratios below are arithmetic on numbers in this file. Each row names the weight. Loadings are mass/area (kg/m²), not newtons. Thrust-to-weight is kgf/kg. The product **16 600 kgf** is not printed anywhere opened; it appears only as `2 × 8300` inside a DERIVED formula. Quotients are the fraction, then the same value rounded to three decimal places. Rounding is not a second source.

Do not subtract empty mass from normal takeoff mass and call the difference fuel. The pages do not say what is in the normal mass (fuel, pilot, ammunition, missiles).

On the booklet table a dot in a mass or a ceiling is a thousands separator: 11.200 кг is 11200 kg, 15.300 кг is 15300 kg, 18.480 кг is 18480 kg, 18.000 м is 18000 m. It is not an English decimal point. The value column uses the expanded integer.

## Engine

Two RD-33. Thrust rows are **per engine** except where the print itself has a «2 х» prefix. No opened page says **стендовая** or **установленная**. H = 0, M = 0 is Klimov’s static point. It is not relabeled “bench” or “installed”.

Klimov’s non-afterburning maximum is the rating English cards call military. The Russian page does not use the word military. The museum engine page calls 5040 kgf **взлётный режим**. The museum aircraft table calls it **максимальная** thrust. Those words are not merged.
