# Rafale C

Exterior block-in numbers for one real **Dassault Rafale C**: the single-seat Armée de l'Air / Armée de l'Air et de l'Espace land jet. Close-coupled canards, side-mounted intakes, one vertical fin, delta wing. Gear-up is the mesh to build. Gear-down is a separate object.

This file records printed figures only. It does not average sources, does not measure photographs, and does not invent stations. Where a loft, station, or angle was looked for and not printed, the cell is **NOT PUBLISHED**.

Live `dassault-aviation.com` HTML returned a block page. The Dassault numbers below are from Wayback captures and from the Paris Air Show 2023 press PDF, both of which returned body text. French Dassault pages are cited with the English ones.

## Variant being modeled

Model the **Rafale C**, single seat, land-based. Dassault’s 2023 press kit calls it “the Air Force single-seat RAFALE C”. The 2015 “A fully optimized airframe” page calls it “the RAFALE C single-seater operated from land bases”. The 2024 French civil-and-military page has a row titled **Rafale Air C (monoplace)**.

Do not build the Rafale B canopy, the Rafale M gear, or the Rafale A demonstrator, and do not move the mould line for F4 or F5. Dassault states that C, B, and M share a common airframe and that naval-versus-land differences are mainly the undercarriage and the arresting hook. That sentence is unsplit: it does not print the size of those differences.

## How to use these numbers in Blender

Units: metres. One Blender unit = 1 m.

- Origin at the nose tip.
- **+X** pilot’s right, **+Y** forward, **+Z** up.
- The airframe lies at **Y ≤ 0**.
- `aft_m` is positive going aft. Blender `Y = -aft_m`.
- Nose datum: `aft_m = 0`, `Y = 0`.
- **up_m datum.** `up_m` is height above the horizontal plane **Z = 0 through the nose origin**. That plane is a modeling waterline, not a published aircraft waterline and not the ground. No opened source says whether “hauteur” is gear-up or gear-down, or whether it runs from the fin tip to the ground, to the lowest fuselage point, or to some other point. The vertical position of the nose tip relative to the fin tip is **NOT PUBLISHED**. Do not put the fin tip at Z = 5.30 m or Z = 5.34 m. Use a published height only as an overall envelope check after you have chosen your own Z datum.

If you block the jet to the current Dassault characteristics length of 15.30 m, the aft end of that length is `aft_m = 15.30`, `Y = -15.30`. Dassault does not say whether that aft point is a nozzle, the fin, or another extremity. The Rafale Air C row’s 15,3 m is the same length at one-decimal precision. The 15,27 m figures are a different printed length. Do not place the tail at 15.285 m or any other average.

Do not scale a Rafale A, a Rafale B, or a Rafale M mesh onto these numbers. A and the production-size airframe are different lengths and spans in the Wikipedia comparisons. B shares the published fuselage length and does not share a published canopy. M shares the Dassault “common airframe” sentence and then differs in the gear and the hook, with no door drawing and no gear-leg length in the Dassault pages opened.

Gear-up is the primary mesh. Gear-down is a second object. Nothing below locates the axles.
