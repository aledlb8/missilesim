# J-20A

Chengdu single-seat canard-delta fighter with diverterless intakes, twin canted all-moving fins, twin engines, and ventral strakes. This note is exterior geometry only.

**No official length, wingspan, or height was found.** Nothing opened from AVIC, Chengdu Aircraft Corporation (CAC), the Chengdu Aircraft Design Institute, or the PLA prints a J-20 or J-20A length, span, or height. The figures below are competing secondary claims. They are not averaged, and none of them is upgraded to OFFICIAL.

Almost every station, chord, sweep, cant, and cross-section is **NOT PUBLISHED**. Where a non-official page prints a sweep or a bay length, it is listed so it is not mistaken for a factory number, then rejected for lofting.

## Variant being modeled

Model the **production single-seat J-20A** in the sense used by CCTV in January 2026: the single-seat upgrade of the J-20, not the 2011 demonstrator and not the twin-seat jet.

CCTV, quoting commentator Zhang Xuefeng, calls both the J-20 and the J-20A single-seat multirole fighters, and calls the J-20A the upgraded one. The only exterior difference that page states is at the canopy: on the earlier jet the canopy sits higher and the all-round view is better; on the J-20A the canopy meets the fuselage more smoothly, and there is a raised bulge where the canopy joins the body. No length, height, or station delta is given. A 澎湃 reprint of CCTV's program *砺剑* says the same thing in fewer words: the most direct visual difference is that the J-20A is raised where the fuselage meets the area behind the cockpit, and that the J-20A and J-20S were publicly shown at the 3 September 2025 parade. That reprint gives no millimetres either.

Do not build any of the following as this mesh:

## How to use these numbers in Blender

- Units are metres.
- Origin at the nose tip. Because no pitot length is published, the nose tip is the forward tip of the radome you model. Do not add a probe ahead of the origin.
- **+X** is the pilot's right, **+Y** is forward, **+Z** is up.
- The airframe stays at **Y ≤ 0**. **aft_m** is positive going aft. Blender **Y = −aft_m**. The nose tip is aft_m = 0 and Y = 0.
- **up_m datum:** up_m is +Z from the nose-tip origin, not from the ground and not from a waterline. No opened source defines a waterline, a ground line, or whether "height" is fin-tip height with the fins parked, gear down, or something else. A published overall height is **not** the fin's up_m. Gear length is unpublished, so do not drop the origin onto a ground plane to force a 4.45 m or 4.69 m overall height.
- Do not scale the model to a single length or span. If a block-in scale is unavoidable, pick one secondary row and label the blend file with that row. Do not mix a 21.2 m length with a 12.88 m span.
- Do not loft sweeps, chords, bay lengths, or canard spans from the tables below. They disagree, and the only table that prints sweeps fails a basic planform check.

## Not published

- Any AVIC, CAC, or PLA length, span, or height, for the J-20 or the J-20A.
- A length, span, or height that belongs specifically to the raised-spine J-20A, and any numeric delta for the spine, canopy, nose, intake, or nozzle.
- Whether placard "机身长" includes a pitot. Pitot length in any case.
- Height datum (ground, static fin, fin parked, gear down).
- Waterline, fuselage-station chart, every cross-section, every radius.
- Wing root and tip chords, thickness, twist, anhedral in degrees, official sweep. The Tsinghua degree row is not a substitute.
- Canard span. The two printed spans (1.50 m and 7.62 m) disagree, and 1.50 m fails against the chords printed beside it.
- Canard dihedral in degrees, pivot station, and J-20A tip clip.
- Fin cant, fin area, fin height as a component, ventral-strake size, and fin-pivot station.
- Inlet capture area, bump dimensions, lip station, nozzle diameter, nozzle length, and which nozzle is on a service J-20A.
- Canopy length, width, and height relative to the fuselage.
- Landing-gear track, wheelbase, tire size, wheel count, and door-tooth count.
- Weapon-bay door length, width, and hinge lines. The 4.50 m and 3.60 m figures are not accepted as door sizes.
- Pylon stations and pylon spacing.
- Light positions, gun presence and muzzle position, antenna map, and optical-window coordinates (two secondary wordings disagree).
- J-20A service paint.
